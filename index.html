Secure & Clear Pathway

The Acquisition Process

How we work with you to guarantee seamless, stress-free property transactions in compliance with Tanzanian Land Acts.

STEP 01

Consultation & Goal Alignment

We sit down or hop on a call to understand your needs: budget, use-case (residential vs. commercial), and preferred neighborhoods in Arusha.

STEP 02

Property Search & Site Visit

We guide you to filtered options and take you on a physical inspection of the boundaries, surveying beacons, and local infrastructure.

STEP 03

Agreement & Reservation

A formal Purchase Offer Letter is drafted. You place a reservation deposit (M-Pesa or Bank) to secure the plot while legal title search occurs.

STEP 04

Closing & Handover

Final payments are cleared, and land transfer documents are submitted to the Arusha Land Registry. We deliver the registered Title Deed directly to you.
