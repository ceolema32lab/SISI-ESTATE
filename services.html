Professional Asset Valuation

Receive certified, accurate property and land valuation reports prepared by registered valuers. Whether you need an official market appraisal for bank financing, corporate accounting, or family estate distribution, our reports meet all regulatory and financial institution standards.

Bank mortgage & loan collateral valuation certificates
Pre-purchase & pre-sale fair market value assessments

03 — SERVICES

Land Surveying & Beaconing

Our licensed land surveyors use high-precision GNSS/RTK equipment to execute cadastral surveys, install permanent boundary beacons, and design master subdivision layouts. We coordinate directly with the Ministry of Lands to register coordinates and issue approved deed plans.

Cadastral boundary surveying and concrete beacon pin placement
Estate subdivision planning and town-planning drawing approvals
Boundary recovery, re-establishment, and dispute resolution
Topographical and engineering site surveys for construction
Enquire about this service
04 — SERVICES

Title Deed Processing & Transfers

Navigating land registry procedures can be time-consuming. We handle the entire paper trail—from official searches and capital gains tax assessments to local government witness clearances and final Certificate of Right of Occupancy issuance in your name.

Official Ministry of Lands registry search & encumbrance due diligence
First-time Certificate of Right of Occupancy (Title Deed) application
End-to-end ownership transfer, stamp duty, and tax clearance coordination
Local government executive (Mwenyekiti / Ward) verification & witnessing
Enquire about this service
