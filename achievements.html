Our Journey

Key Milestones Across 10 Years of Growth

Tracing our path from an ambitious land survey startup in Arusha to a premier regional land development powerhouse.

2016 • Inception

Incorporation of Sisi Estate

Sisi Estate Company Limited was founded in Arusha with a single core mission: eliminating fraudulent land sales by providing 100% verified, legal surveyed plots.

2018 • Landmark Project

The Mkonoo Residential Block

Successfully delivered our first major 120-plot residential master plan in Mkonoo with tarmac access, beacon certifications, and municipal water connections.

2021 • Strategic Expansion

USA River & Kisongo Corridors

Expanded acquisitions along key arterial routes: scenic residential estate land in USA River and prime commercial highway parcels in Kisongo near Arusha Airport.

2024 • Digital & Diaspora Desk

Drone Inspections & Remote Transfers

Introduced high-definition 4K drone viewings and dedicated diaspora legal escrow services, enabling Tanzanians abroad to purchase land safely without travelling.

2026 • Flagship Release

Njiro Block HH & 1,200 Titles

Celebrated our 1,200th verified title deed milestone and launched the prestigious Njiro Block HH master development, solidifying our regional leadership.
